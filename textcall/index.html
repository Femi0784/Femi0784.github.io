    TextCall

    Silent for you.
    Loud for them.

    Type a message and TextCall phones the person, speaking it in your own voice. Made for moments when you can't talk but they need to hear you: church, meetings, hospitals, or a sleeping baby.

    1. Set up your voice by reading a short script.
    2. Pick a contact, type or tap a quick message.
    3. Watch the call happen, and play the recipient using the phone on the call screen.

    Prototype only. It uses your browser's built-in voice instead of a cloned one, and simulates the call. A real version needs a calling service and a voice-cloning service behind it.
